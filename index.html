About the artist

I have been creating fractal and geometric art for many years. It has always been my goal to transport viewers, including myself, into deeper worlds. I hope people gain a new perspective after viewing my art. There is so much more to our world than what is material.

Through kaleidoscopic creations, I hope to capture people's attention by inviting them to fall into the image. Look deeply, let all peripherals disappear. Become enveloped by the colors and patterns and give your mind permission to see. — Gael
