Gallery and Artist Statement

Take a dive into Gael's Fractal Gallery, an online showcase of mesmerizing fractal and geometric art. Browse elaborate patters, vibrant colors, and infiinte detail that draw you into the depth and complexities of a mathematical imagination.
Through kalediscopic creations, I hope to capture peoples attention by inviting them to fall into the image. Look deeply, let all peripherals disappear. Become enveloped by the colors and patterns and give your mind permisison to see . ~ Gael ~

About the Artist

I have been creating fractal / geometric art for many years. It has always been my goal to transport viewers, including myself, into deeper worlds. I hope people gain a new perspective after viewing my art, there is so much more to our world than what is material.
